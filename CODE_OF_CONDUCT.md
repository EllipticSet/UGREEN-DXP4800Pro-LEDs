# Code of conduct

## Expected behavior

Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests and other project spaces. Welcome people with different backgrounds and experience. Discuss technical decisions on their merits, accept good-faith feedback and respect others' privacy.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, sexual harassment, deliberate disruption and publication of another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

These rules apply to this repository and to public spaces where someone represents the project.

The repository owner may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions and restrict participation. The response should reflect the severity and repetition of the behavior. People affected by a moderation decision may request reconsideration without repeating the disruptive behavior.

## Reporting

For inappropriate content on GitHub, use the content's **Report content** option. See [GitHub's reporting guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

For routine moderation concerns, an issue may identify the relevant public discussion without repeating abusive material or disclosing private information. Use GitHub's reporting channel for sensitive concerns. The security reporting channel is reserved for software vulnerabilities.

Reports are considered as time permits. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
